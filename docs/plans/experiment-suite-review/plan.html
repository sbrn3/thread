Problem & Intent

Status, owner & next action

Draft, waiting on the owner's approval. On 2026-10-10 the owner asked whether the experiment suite still fits its purpose ("I'm worried it overcomplicates things and messes with the user experience"). This plan came out of /grill; see grill-summary.md → Confirmed understanding. Next action: once the owner approves, S00 rebases this branch and starts the single merge-and-deploy run in exec.md.

Outcome and constraints

The owner couldn't tell what the next-day probe was, sitting next to their own memory passages. Their daily reading had also shrunk to a few verses with no explanation, and most of their misses come from life, such as camping or a change of roster. The outcome: a reading app first. Research is something a reader opts into, and the reading never changes without the reader seeing why.

  • Experiments is off by default and off on every existing install. The owner's phone starts clean.
  • With it off, nothing is learned or randomized, and the six behaviours are plain settings.
  • With it on, the app learns only from experiments, and every conclusion is a suggestion the reader applies.
  • Constraints from AGENTS.md:
    • events is append-only;
    • no migration, and none is needed;
    • seeded PRNG only;
    • /src/lab never imports /src/ui;
    • reconcile() stays idempotent and replay-correct;
    • everything stays on the device.

Scope and non-goals

In scope:

  • the Experiments switch, with on/off state recorded as events;
  • a behaviours resolver, so every reversal actually applies its arm;
  • gating for the reversals, SRBAI, reports, E6, E10 and the bandit;
  • removing the E9 probe, with E10 scored on sealing alone;
  • the visible dose ladder and "Read the whole chapter", plus a fix for resuming a sitting;
  • a fixed nudge when off, the future schedule reset on toggle, and nudge delivery recorded;
  • a year-review verdict for readers who never answered SRBAI;
  • the knot's Reading settings and Experiments rows (direction C);
  • docs, website copy and a release.

Not in scope: the rest of the lapse ladder (one question, off-ramp, dormancy and partner hand-off are unchanged), marking planned time away (declined at grill), sitting-length policy, and a component renderer for tests.

Evidence and decisions

  • Why the reading shrank. diagnose() in src/lab/steps.ts steps meta.dose down a rung on day 2 of every lapse, and back up only after a 7-day sealed streak. todaysTarget in src/lab/dose.ts gives that rung priority over everything else. Nothing on screen said so.
  • One-verse sittings. At that rung, splitSittings(chapter, 1) in src/text/sittings.ts cuts a chapter into roughly one sitting per verse. The stored current_sitting index then points at the wrong verses when the target changes, because session.load only clamps it. This matches the 11 sittings for James 4 that reading-screen-and-motion noticed in the owner's data.
  • E1, E3 and E4 never applied their arms. Flow.tsx (~669) reads seal, floor and streakVisible from profile only. Only E7 (notifier.ts e7ArmBActive) and E11/E12 (arrivalVisibility.ts) read the active phase.
  • The nudge trial couldn't learn. Nothing set delivered = 1 for a sent nudge. cancelToday voided nudges that had already fired before the seal. session.seal hard-codes before_nudge: 1.
  • E6's re_entry arm has no consumer. It stays behind the switch.
  • The year review. verdictFor in analysis/yearReview.ts calls any reader with no SRBAI data "compliance, not a habit yet".
  • The probe is safe to remove. weightedPick is keyed per call, so no other seeded draw moves. The probes table and the historical event types stay.
  • Decisions. These come from the grill (rounds 1–5), the JOURNAL Decision 2026-10-10, and the owner's /plan answers on 2026-10-10:
    • probe removal goes before S06 (moot now that S06 has landed);
    • the mechanic-friction switch to tap is offered, never applied silently;
    • changing a tested setting ends that test;
    • with Experiments off, a nudge goes out on every unread day in the cue's wording;
    • knot direction C;
    • the Smart Review is authorized;
    • merge and deploy happen in one run.
  • Calls the planner made, for review at approval:
    • the lab clock starts the day after switching on, with no 21-day baseline;
    • E10 and the bandit count from the same start;
    • completed experiments keep their reports when switched back on;
    • an applied suggestion stays as a setting when Experiments is turned off;
    • a reader's choice retires that experiment for good, so the queue never restarts a test the reader ended;
    • cue strength counts only days from this build on, because older days all carry a hard-coded "before the nudge".

Requirements (confirmed)

User stories

  1. As a reader, I want the app to run no experiments unless I turn Experiments on, so that it's simply a reading app.
  2. As a reader with Experiments off, I want to see How often, What counts as read, Sealing, Streak, Day count and Sitting count in Reading settings, at today's defaults, and change any of them, so that nothing about my reading is hidden.
  3. As a reader, I want one line explaining what Experiments does (3-week tests, a monthly question, suggestions) before I turn it on.
  4. As a reader with Experiments on, I want to see what's being tested, until when, and what comes next, so that changes never surprise me.
  5. As a reader, I want a finished experiment to offer a suggestion with its reason, which I can apply or keep at the seal or later in the knot. Nothing should change until I apply it.
  6. As a reader, I want changing a setting that's being tested to end that test, so that my choice always wins.
  7. As a reader, I don't want the next-day "what do you remember" probe any more. My memory passages are where I practise remembering.
  8. As the lab (a silent actor), I want E10 to score dose arms on sealing alone, so that it still works without the probe.
  9. As a reader whose reading was shortened after missed days, I want one line saying so and why.
  10. As that reader, I want "Read the whole chapter" to give me the rest of today's chapter from the next unread verse, and to put the dose ladder back at the top.
  11. As a reader on an E10 dose day, I want the note to say the length is part of an experiment, with the same option.
  12. As a reader whose dose changes mid-chapter, I want to resume at the next unread verse, neither re-reading nor skipping.
  13. As the lab, I want every reversal (E1, E3, E4, E7, E11, E12) to apply its arm while active, so that verdicts measure real differences.
  14. As the owner, I want my phone to start with Experiments off, the in-progress phase stopped and no prompts, with the old rows kept.
  15. As the lab, I want switching on again to restart the queue from the first experiment without a report, so that a stopped phase is never resumed half-way.
  16. As the lab, I want on/off recorded in the append-only log and read per day, so that reconcile() replay stays exact.
  17. As a reader who never answers the monthly check-in, I want a fair year review, not "compliance, not a habit".
  18. As a reader with Experiments off, I want no monthly SRBAI prompt and no phase report after the seal.
  19. As a reader with Experiments off, I want one nudge at my nudge hour on any day I haven't sealed, in the cue's words, and never more than one a day.
  20. As a reader, I want switching Experiments on or off to rebuild my upcoming nudges, so that a leftover experimental silent day can't swallow a reminder.
  21. As the lab, I want each nudge's delivery recorded (from the OS schedule at the next open, and from a received notification while the app is open), so that the nudge trial and the bandit can learn.
  22. As the lab, I want a nudge that fired before the seal to count as delivered, and only a nudge cancelled by an earlier seal to be voided.
  23. As a screen-reader user, I want the switch, the choice chips and Read the whole chapter labelled, at least 44 pt, and in reading order.
  24. As an offline reader, I want all of this to work with no network. It reads only local SQLite and the bundled WEB text.
  25. As a reader whose holds keep getting cancelled, I want to be asked "switch to tap?" before I read, with Switch or Keep hold, instead of being switched without asking.

Smart Review

An independent read-only reviewer checked the draft exec.md against the code on 2026-10-10. It reported 6 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM and 2 LOW findings. All are resolved in exec.md, and one needed an owner decision.

FindingResolution
HIGH 1: a nudge marked delivered after its day is closed never gets a reward, because attributeRewards and updateBandit only touch the date being closed.S05: a late-reward sweep covers earlier delivered = 1 rows with no reward, scoring each against its own day. Deliveries are recorded before reconcile on every open and every return to the foreground.
HIGH 2: inferring delivery would mark every past cancelled nudge as delivered, and also any nudge after permission was revoked.Each new row stores ctx.triggerAt, and inference applies only to rows that have it. A one-time pass sets old 0 rows to -1, and inference is skipped without permission.
HIGH 3: changing a setting under test restarts the same test, because "first experiment with no report" picks it again.A setting_changed event after an experiment's start retires that experiment. "Next" is defined exactly in Shared facts.
HIGH 4: keying completion on reports lets an all-disturbed experiment be reseeded over its done rows.An experiment counts as complete once phase 3 is done, and seedPhase never overwrites a done row.
HIGH 5: resuming from a verse number could skip a merged-forward chapter.The position is stored as chapter:verse. A rebuilt remainder sets portionChapters = [chapter] and never merges forward, and there's a test for a short chapter.
HIGH 6: reading-motion S06 had already merged, and v0.12.0 was tagged, so S01 and S02 targeted files that had moved.S01 and S02 are rewritten against beforeList.ts, BeforeYouRead.tsx, test/beforeList.test.ts and the new ArrivalZone header. The ordering dependency is dropped and the release is now v0.13.0.
MED: S03 doesn't typecheck on its own, because of the new getPendingReport parameter.The parameter is optional.
MED: before_nudge is decided after the seal is written, and the app doesn't re-sync when it comes back to the foreground.nudgeStateToday is checked before the seal event, and an AppState hook re-runs it on return.
MED: real before_nudge values would make old history look like cue_collapse.Cue strength counts only from meta.cue_strength_since.
MED: the mechanic-friction override would lose to the E1 arm, and with Experiments off it learns from everyday use.Owner decided 2026-10-10: offer it, don't do it. It becomes a "switch to tap?" lapse row (story 25, S03 and S04).
MED: "the cue hour at row time" isn't the real trigger time.The stored triggerAt and "still scheduled" are the source of truth.

Verification

Automated gates:

  • Each slice passes its focused tests, npm test, npm run typecheck and CI on its PR.
  • An independent audit checks the final diff against this plan and exec.md. HIGH and MEDIUM findings are fixed before the tag.

Notification scheduling changes, so this checklist is mandatory. Run it on the dev client only, with synthetic or restored data. The steps are in exec.md S07.

  • Upgrade over v0.12 data → Experiments is Off, no SRBAI or report prompt appears, and active phases are stopped.
  • Off, unsealed, nudge 2 minutes ahead → one notification in the cue's words. On reopen, its row has delivered = 1.
  • Seal before the nudge → no notification. The row has delivered = -1.
  • Seal after the nudge → delivered = 1 and sealed_before_nudge = 0.
  • Turn Experiments on → the future window is rebuilt with at most one nudge a day, and "Now testing: How often" shows from tomorrow.
  • Turn it off again → the window is rebuilt with no silent days, and the phase is stopped.
  • Change Sealing while it's being tested → the test ends, and the next experiment starts at the next reconcile.
  • Lapse at one verse → the note shows. "Read the whole chapter" gives the rest of the chapter from the next unread verse and logs one dose_reset.
  • Notification permission revoked → no crash, nothing scheduled, nothing marked delivered.
  • Holds cancelled above 15% (synthetic data) → a "switch to tap?" row appears before reading; Keep hold changes nothing, and Switch sets tap with "You chose this".
